  \begin{quote}
    \small \textbf{Abstract.---} We study the metal-insulator transition in three-dimensional (3D) quantum percolation on simple cubic (SC) and body-centered cubic (BCC) lattices. Using the Transfer Matrix Method (TMM), we compute the dimensionless localization length $\Lambda_M$ for system sizes up to $M=11$. On the simple cubic lattice, we validate our numerical framework against existing high-precision results at non-zero energy $E = 0.1$, obtaining a critical threshold $p_q \approx 0.4538$ and critical exponent $\nu \approx 1.62$, which is consistent with the 3D Orthogonal (AI) class. At the band center $E = 0.0$, we find $p_q \approx 0.591$ and $\nu \approx 1.99$, which is consistent with the Chiral Orthogonal (BDI) symmetry class. For the body-centered cubic lattice, we overcome the connectivity matrix singularity by simulating odd transverse system widths $M$, yielding a quantum percolation threshold of $p_q \approx 0.315$ and a critical exponent $\nu \approx 1.59$. The lower threshold of the BCC lattice is attributed to its higher coordination number ($Z=8$).
  \end{quote}
