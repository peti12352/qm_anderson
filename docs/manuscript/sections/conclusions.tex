\section{Conclusions and Outlook}
\label{sec:conclusions}

In summary, we have investigated the 3D quantum percolation transition on both Simple Cubic (SC) and Body-Centered Cubic (BCC) lattices using a modular, high-statistics implementation of the Transfer Matrix Method (TMM). 

% On the SC lattice, we successfully validated our numerical methods by reproducing the known critical threshold $p_q \approx XXX$ and critical exponent $\nu \approx XXX$ of the 3D Orthogonal class at energy $E = 0.1$. 
% At $E = 0.0$, we observed a transition at $p_q \approx XXX$ with a critical exponent $\nu \approx XXX$, indicating that the chiral symmetry present at the band center places the system in the Chiral Orthogonal (BDI) symmetry class.

% For the BCC lattice, we resolved the connectivity matrix singularity issue at even widths by conducting simulations on odd transverse widths $M \in \{3, 5, 7, 9, 11\}$. At $E = 0.1$, we found the quantum percolation threshold to be $p_q(\text{BCC}) \approx 0.315$, which is lower than the SC threshold due to the higher coordination number ($Z=8$ versus $Z=6$). The critical exponent $\nu \approx 1.59$ remains consistent with the 3D Orthogonal class, confirming that coordination geometry does not affect the universality class.
